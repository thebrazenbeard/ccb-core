# Commercial Licensing

CCB Core is source-visible but is not offered under an open-source license. Public availability does not grant commercial exploitation rights.

A separate written commercial license is required before using original repository material to generate revenue or commercial advantage, including resale, paid deployment, incorporation into a commercial product or service, hosted or managed service operation, commercial sublicensing, or monetized derivative works.

To request commercial permission, contact the repository owner through GitHub and identify the version, intended use, distribution model, and expected commercial scope.

No permission should be inferred from silence, repository visibility, access to a clone, issue discussion, pull-request review, or prior noncommercial permission.
